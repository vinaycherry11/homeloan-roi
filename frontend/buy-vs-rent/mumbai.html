How to read this

A home pays you back in two ways: the rent you no longer pay and the rise in its price. In Mumbai the rent part is about 4.0% a year, well below a 8.5% home loan, so price growth has to do most of the work. Where a locality’s past growth sits above the “buying wins above” figure, buying has come out ahead; where it sits below, renting and investing the difference in a Nifty 50 index fund has.

These are typical flats and past trends. Your own flat, rent and loan rate can change the answer, so run your numbers in the calculator.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Mumbai?

It depends on the locality. In our 2026 model of a typical 2BHK, buying comes out ahead in 0 of 9 Mumbai localities if prices keep rising at their 10-year trend. Buying wins where prices have grown faster than about 9.0% a year, the median break-even here.

What is the rental yield in Mumbai?

Gross rental yields for a typical 2BHK in our Mumbai localities are around 4.0% (median), so a home costs about 25 years of rent.

Where does buying make the most sense in Mumbai?

Kharghar, where the 10-year price trend (6.9%) is furthest above the growth buying needs. Renting and investing is strongest in Andheri East.
