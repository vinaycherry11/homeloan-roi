The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Padi

Padi is a north-west Chennai neighbourhood near Anna Nagar and the Ambattur industrial estate. It has older industrial land being redeveloped into housing and retail, and is close to Anna Nagar's schools and shops.

  • Near Anna Nagar
  • Ambattur industry
  • Industrial-to-residential shift
  • Affordable
  • Inner Ring Road access

Major employers nearby: Ambattur Industrial Estate, Offices in Anna Nagar, Local manufacturing.

Getting around

  • Highway: Inner Ring Road; Chennai–Tiruvallur High Road
  • Peak traffic: Inner Ring Road and Padi junction are busy at peaks
  • Congestion: Moderate

Investment view

Padi offers proximity to Anna Nagar at much lower prices. Growth has been modest. 2BHK rents run about ₹12,000–14,000/mo. Suits buyers working in Ambattur or Anna Nagar.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Padi?

Even if prices keep rising at Padi’s 10-year trend of 5.5% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.43 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.6% a year. Padi’s 10-year trend is 4.1 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹45 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Padi?

About 3.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹13,000 a month on a home worth about ₹45 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 29, meaning the home costs 29 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Padi?

About ₹31,242 a month on a ₹36 L loan (20% down on a ₹45 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹20,117 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Padi?

About 5.5% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 3.9% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
