The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Thoraipakkam

Thoraipakkam sits in the middle of Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR), Chennai's IT Expressway, between Perungudi and Sholinganallur. It is surrounded by IT parks and has a dense mix of apartments, PG housing and gated communities. The Phase 2 metro along OMR is under construction.

  • OMR IT corridor
  • Walk-to-work housing
  • Gated communities
  • Phase 2 metro (upcoming)
  • Strong rental demand

Major employers nearby: Tech parks along OMR (Cognizant, TCS, Accenture and others), RMZ & Prestige tech parks, Startups & BPOs.

Getting around

  • Metro: Phase 2 Corridor 3 (Madhavaram–SIPCOT) along OMR under construction; Perungudi MRTS ~3 km
  • Highway: OMR; Pallavaram–Thoraipakkam Radial Road links to GST Road
  • Peak traffic: OMR is congested 8:30–10:30 am and 6–9 pm, especially near junctions with Pallavaram–Thoraipakkam Radial Road
  • Congestion: High

Schools

  • Schools along OMR (Various) — Several CBSE, ICSE and international schools within a few km

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Hospitals along OMR (Apollo, Gleneagles nearby), Hospitals in Perungudi & Sholinganallur
  • Malls: Retail along OMR, Phoenix Marketcity, Velachery (~6 km)
  • Parks: Pallikaranai Marsh (~3 km), ECR beaches (~4 km)

Investment view

Thoraipakkam has some of Chennai's deepest rental demand thanks to its location in the middle of the OMR job belt. The upcoming metro should ease the commute. 2BHK rents run about ₹22,000–26,000/mo. A good pick for IT workers and for buyers who want easy-to-let homes.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Thoraipakkam?

At Thoraipakkam’s 10-year price trend of 8.8% a year, the two choices end up within ₹12.4 L of each other after 20 years (renting slightly ahead). Small changes in price growth, rent or your loan rate decide it. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.0% a year. Thoraipakkam’s 10-year trend is 0.2 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹80 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Thoraipakkam?

About 3.6% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹24,000 a month on a home worth about ₹80 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 28, meaning the home costs 28 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Thoraipakkam?

About ₹55,541 a month on a ₹64 L loan (20% down on a ₹80 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹34,874 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Thoraipakkam?

About 8.8% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 6.0% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
