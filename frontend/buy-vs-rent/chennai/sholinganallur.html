The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Sholinganallur

Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR) is Chennai's primary IT corridor, stretching ~45 km from Madhya Kailash to Siruseri SIPCOT. The northern stretch (Perungudi–Sholinganallur) is the densest IT zone, housing Tidel Park, SIPCOT IT Park, and campuses of Cognizant, Infosys, TCS, and Wipro. Chennai Metro Phase 2 Corridor 3 will bring metro stations to Perungudi, Sholinganallur, and beyond from 2026, which is expected to be transformative for this car-dependent corridor.

  • Chennai's primary 45 km IT corridor
  • Tidel Park & SIPCOT IT Park
  • Metro Phase 2 Corridor 3 pipeline
  • Coastal proximity (ECR 3 km)
  • Premium residential stock 2020–2025

Major employers nearby: Cognizant Technology Solutions (Sholinganallur), Infosys Chennai, TCS (SIPCOT), Wipro (Sholinganallur), CGI Group, Hexaware Technologies.

Getting around

  • Highway: East Coast Road (ECR) — 3 km via Sholinganallur; Chennai Port–Maduravoyal Expressway — 15 km; NH-32 — 12 km
  • Peak traffic: Severely congested on OMR from Perungudi to Sholinganallur 8:30–10:30 am and 6–9 pm; corridor is heavily car-dependent with few alternatives

Investment view

OMR delivers 8–11% appreciation with the strongest fundamentals in Chennai — largest IT workforce catchment and Phase 2 metro as a near-term catalyst. Rental yields at 4–5% with 2BHK rents of ₹22,000–40,000/mo. Metro Phase 2 opening (from 2026) will be the biggest price inflection point for the corridor in a decade.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Sholinganallur?

At Sholinganallur’s 10-year price trend of 8.3% a year, the two choices end up within ₹21.2 L of each other after 20 years (buying slightly ahead). Small changes in price growth, rent or your loan rate decide it. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.0% a year. Sholinganallur’s 10-year trend is 0.3 points above that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹75 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Sholinganallur?

About 4.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹28,000 a month on a home worth about ₹75 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 22, meaning the home costs 22 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Sholinganallur?

About ₹52,069 a month on a ₹60 L loan (20% down on a ₹75 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹27,194 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Sholinganallur?

About 8.3% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.7% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
