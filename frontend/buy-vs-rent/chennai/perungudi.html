The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Perungudi

Perungudi is at the start of OMR, just south of Velachery and Taramani, next to TIDEL Park and the early IT parks of Chennai. It has the MRTS line, a mix of older housing and new apartments, and quick access to both the IT corridor and central Chennai.

  • OMR gateway
  • Near TIDEL Park
  • MRTS access
  • Close to Velachery
  • Mixed housing

Major employers nearby: TIDEL Park, Tech parks at Taramani & Perungudi, Offices along OMR.

Getting around

  • Metro: MRTS: Perungudi station; Phase 2 Corridor 3 along OMR under construction
  • Highway: OMR; Velachery Main Road; ECR via Thiruvanmiyur
  • Peak traffic: OMR near Perungudi toll and the Velachery link are busy in peaks
  • Congestion: High

Schools

Nearby

  • Malls: Phoenix Marketcity, Velachery (~3 km), Grand Square Mall, Velachery
  • Parks: Pallikaranai Marsh (~3 km), Guindy National Park (~8 km)

Investment view

Perungudi offers good access to both the city and the IT corridor, which keeps it in demand with tenants. 2BHK rents run about ₹20,000–24,000/mo. A balanced choice for buyers who work at the northern end of OMR.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Perungudi?

Even if prices keep rising at Perungudi’s 10-year trend of 8.0% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹85.6 L richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.2% a year. Perungudi’s 10-year trend is 1.2 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹75 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Perungudi?

About 3.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹22,000 a month on a home worth about ₹75 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 28, meaning the home costs 28 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Perungudi?

About ₹52,069 a month on a ₹60 L loan (20% down on a ₹75 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹33,194 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Perungudi?

About 8.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.3% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
