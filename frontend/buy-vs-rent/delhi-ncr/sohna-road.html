The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Sohna Road

Sohna Road is Gurgaon's fastest-growing mid-to-premium residential and commercial corridor, stretching south from NH-48 towards Sohna. It offers more affordable prices than Golf Course Road while sharing proximity to Cyber City and the Bhiwadi–Neemrana industrial belt. Large integrated townships and plotted developments dominate. The Southern Peripheral Road (SPR) extension has sharply improved connectivity.

  • Affordable Cyber City fringe
  • Large integrated township belt
  • SPR & NH-248A connectivity
  • Mid-premium IT professional corridor
  • Rapid price appreciation 2022–2025

Major employers nearby: DLF Cyber City (7 km), Unitech Cyber Park (5 km), Vatika Business Park, Bestech Business Tower, Maruti Suzuki (Manesar — 15 km).

Investment view

Sohna Road offers 10–13% appreciation at 25–30% lower entry prices than Golf Course Road, making it Gurgaon's best value IT corridor investment. Rental yields at 4–5% are among NCR's better yielding micro-markets. SPR-driven connectivity improvement is sustaining strong demand momentum.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Sohna Road?

Even if prices keep rising at Sohna Road’s 10-year trend of 7.5% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹48.7 L richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.0% a year. Sohna Road’s 10-year trend is 0.5 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.20 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Sohna Road?

About 4.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹45,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.20 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 22, meaning the home costs 22 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Sohna Road?

About ₹83,311 a month on a ₹96 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.20 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹43,311 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Sohna Road?

About 7.5% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.5% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
