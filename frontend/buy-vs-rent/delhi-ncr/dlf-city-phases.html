The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About DLF City Phases

DLF Cyber City is Gurgaon's premier IT and corporate district — a 125-acre Grade A office campus hosting Fortune 500 companies including Google, Microsoft, EY, Deloitte, PwC, Oracle, and American Express. It is the densest concentration of high-value white-collar employment in North India. Residential demand from Cyber City employees drives the entire Golf Course Road–NH-48 corridor. The Yellow Line metro serves the area via Cyber City Station.

  • North India's largest IT corporate campus
  • Fortune 500 company cluster
  • Yellow Line metro served
  • Golf Course Road premium residential proximity
  • 125-acre Grade A office park

Major employers nearby: Google India, Microsoft India, Deloitte, PwC India, EY India, Oracle India, American Express India.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in DLF City Phases?

Even if prices keep rising at DLF City Phases’s 10-year trend of 8.1% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.35 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.9% a year. DLF City Phases’s 10-year trend is 0.8 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.95 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in DLF City Phases?

About 4.0% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹65,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.95 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 25, meaning the home costs 25 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in DLF City Phases?

About ₹1,35,380 a month on a ₹1.56 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.95 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹78,505 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in DLF City Phases?

About 8.1% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
