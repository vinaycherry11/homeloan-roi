The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Raj Nagar Extension

Raj Nagar Extension is a large budget housing area in Ghaziabad on the NH-58 (Meerut Road). It has many high-rise societies offering some of NCR's lowest prices. Connectivity has improved with the Delhi–Meerut Expressway and the RRTS, though it remains away from the main job centres.

  • Budget high-rises
  • Large township supply
  • Delhi–Meerut Expressway
  • RRTS (Namo Bharat) nearby
  • Ghaziabad suburbs

Major employers nearby: Ghaziabad industry, Offices in Noida & east Delhi (commute).

Getting around

  • Metro: Red Line: Shaheed Sthal (New Bus Adda) ~5 km; Namo Bharat (RRTS) Ghaziabad stations nearby
  • Highway: Delhi–Meerut Expressway ~3 km; NH-58
  • Peak traffic: Meerut Road junctions slow at peaks
  • Congestion: Moderate

  • Parks: Hindon river side, Society parks

Investment view

Raj Nagar Extension is cheap but has a lot of supply and slow price growth. Rents are low. 2BHK-sized rents run about ₹12,000–15,000/mo. Best for budget buyers who will live in the home.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Raj Nagar Extension?

Even if prices keep rising at Raj Nagar Extension’s 10-year trend of 5.1% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹2.36 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 10.1% a year. Raj Nagar Extension’s 10-year trend is 5.0 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹60 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Raj Nagar Extension?

About 2.8% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹14,000 a month on a home worth about ₹60 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 36, meaning the home costs 36 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Raj Nagar Extension?

About ₹41,656 a month on a ₹48 L loan (20% down on a ₹60 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹30,156 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Raj Nagar Extension?

About 5.1% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.4% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
