The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Vasant Kunj

Vasant Kunj is a premium residential area in south-west Delhi, between the airport and Mehrauli. It is known for DDA housing pockets, large flats, green surroundings and luxury malls. Supply is limited and most homes change hands through resale.

  • South Delhi premium
  • Near IGI Airport
  • Green surroundings
  • Luxury malls
  • DDA housing pockets

Major employers nearby: Offices in Aerocity & Gurgaon, JNU & institutions, Central Delhi (via NH-48).

Investment view

Vasant Kunj is a stable, high-value address with limited supply. Yields are low as in most of south Delhi. 2BHK-sized rents run about ₹60,000–75,000/mo for larger flats. Best for buyers who want a long-term south Delhi home.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Vasant Kunj?

Even if prices keep rising at Vasant Kunj’s 10-year trend of 6.0% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹8.39 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.9% a year. Vasant Kunj’s 10-year trend is 3.9 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹2.60 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Vasant Kunj?

About 3.1% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹68,000 a month on a home worth about ₹2.60 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 32, meaning the home costs 32 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Vasant Kunj?

About ₹1,80,507 a month on a ₹2.08 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹2.60 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹1,23,341 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Vasant Kunj?

About 6.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 3.9% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
