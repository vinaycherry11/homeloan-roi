The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Dwarka Sectors

Dwarka is Delhi's largest planned residential township, developed by DDA across 29 sectors on the south-west fringe of the capital. It has excellent Blue Line metro connectivity to Connaught Place and Noida, direct access to IGI Airport via NH-48, and a large, stable middle-class and government employee population. The upcoming Dwarka Expressway (fully operational from 2024) has sharply boosted connectivity to Gurgaon.

  • Delhi's largest DDA planned township
  • Blue Line metro connectivity
  • Airport corridor (15 min to IGI)
  • Dwarka Expressway to Gurgaon
  • Stable government employee community

Major employers nearby: IGI Airport ecosystem (15 min), Aerocity business district (10 km), Gurgaon IT belt via Dwarka Expressway (20 min), Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) campus.

Getting around

  • Metro: Multiple Blue Line stations across Dwarka (Sector 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 21 — Blue Line terminus); direct to Connaught Place, Noida
  • Highway: NH-48 (Delhi–Jaipur) — 1 km; Dwarka Expressway (to Gurgaon) — 2 km; IGI Airport — 12 km
  • Peak traffic: Moderate to heavy on Dwarka Sector 10 Road and Palam Road during peak hours; airport traffic on NH-48 can cause delays
  • Congestion: Moderate

Schools

  • DPS Dwarka (Delhi Public School) (CBSE) — Large DPS campus; one of Dwarka's most reputed schools
  • Kendriya Vidyalaya Dwarka (CBSE) — Central government school; excellent quality at modest fees
  • Ryan International School Dwarka (CBSE) — Well-attended Ryan campus in Dwarka

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Venkateshwar Hospital Dwarka (2 km), Manipal Hospital Dwarka (3 km), AIIMS Trauma Centre (10 km)
  • Malls: Pacific Mall Dwarka (3 km), Vegas Mall Dwarka (2 km), Ambience Mall Gurgaon (12 km)
  • Parks: Sector 10 & 23 DDA Parks (local), Asola Bhatti Wildlife Sanctuary (15 km)

Investment view

Dwarka offers solid 8–10% appreciation driven by airport proximity, Dwarka Expressway opening, and Blue Line metro. Rental yields at 3.5–4.5% are steady from government and airline sector tenants. Aerocity business district growth is the key demand catalyst. One of Delhi's best value-for-money residential investments.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Dwarka Sectors?

Even if prices keep rising at Dwarka Sectors’s 10-year trend of 5.1% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹3.75 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.2% a year. Dwarka Sectors’s 10-year trend is 4.1 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.30 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Dwarka Sectors?

About 4.0% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹43,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.30 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 25, meaning the home costs 25 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Dwarka Sectors?

About ₹90,254 a month on a ₹1.04 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.30 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹52,670 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Dwarka Sectors?

About 5.1% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 3.9% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
