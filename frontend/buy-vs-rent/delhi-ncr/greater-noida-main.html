About Greater Noida (Main)

Greater Noida is a planned city south-east of Noida with wide roads, large sectors, universities and industrial areas. Pari Chowk is its main commercial hub. It offers spacious homes at moderate prices, connected to Noida by the Aqua Line metro and the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway.

  • Planned city
  • Wide roads & green belts
  • Universities
  • Aqua Line metro
  • Moderate prices

Major employers nearby: Industrial sectors (Ecotech, Udyog Vihar), Universities & colleges, Offices in Noida (~20 km).

Getting around

  • Metro: Aqua Line: Pari Chowk, Knowledge Park II, Alpha 1, Delta 1 and other stations
  • Highway: Noida–Greater Noida Expressway; Yamuna Expressway; Eastern Peripheral Expressway
  • Peak traffic: Light inside Greater Noida; the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway is busy at peaks
  • Congestion: Low

Schools

  • Schools in Greater Noida (Various) — Many CBSE schools including DPS and Ryan branches
  • Sharda & Galgotias universities (University) — Large campuses in the city

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Sharda Hospital, Yatharth Hospital, GIMS
  • Malls: Grand Venice Mall, Retail around Pari Chowk
  • Parks: City Park, Greater Noida, Surajpur wetland

Investment view

Greater Noida offers space and planning at low prices, but job density is lower than Noida, so rents are modest. 2BHK-sized rents run about ₹15,000–19,000/mo. Suits families who work in Noida or Greater Noida and want a larger home.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Greater Noida (Main)?

Even if prices keep rising at Greater Noida (Main)’s 10-year trend of 6.9% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.79 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.8% a year. Greater Noida (Main)’s 10-year trend is 2.9 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹70 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Greater Noida (Main)?

About 2.9% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹17,000 a month on a home worth about ₹70 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 34, meaning the home costs 34 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Greater Noida (Main)?

About ₹48,598 a month on a ₹56 L loan (20% down on a ₹70 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹34,515 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Greater Noida (Main)?

About 6.9% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.5% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
