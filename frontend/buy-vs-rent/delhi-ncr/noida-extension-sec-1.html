About Noida Extension (Sec 1)

Noida Extension (Greater Noida West) is a large cluster of high-rise societies between Noida and Greater Noida. Prices well below Noida made it NCR's biggest affordable housing market, though it went through years of project delays. Most towers are now delivered and occupied, and a metro link has been approved.

  • Affordable high-rises
  • Large occupied societies
  • Close to Noida sectors
  • Metro link approved
  • Gaur City & other townships

Major employers nearby: Offices in Noida Sector 62 & 63, Noida Expressway IT parks, Greater Noida industries.

Getting around

  • Metro: No metro yet; Aqua Line extension to Greater Noida West approved; nearest station Noida Sector 51/52 (~8 km)
  • Highway: Noida–Greater Noida link roads; NH-24 (Delhi–Meerut Expressway) ~6 km
  • Peak traffic: Gaur Chowk and Char Murti roundabout are heavily congested at peaks
  • Congestion: High

Schools

  • Schools in Greater Noida West (Various) — Several CBSE schools inside and near the societies

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Yatharth Hospital, Greater Noida West, Local hospitals
  • Malls: Gaur City Mall, Retail along Gaur Chowk
  • Parks: Society parks, Hindon river side

Investment view

Noida Extension has lots of supply, which keeps price growth and rents moderate, but it offers some of the cheapest new homes near Noida. 2BHK-sized rents run about ₹16,000–20,000/mo. A practical first home for buyers working in Noida.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Noida Extension (Sec 1)?

Even if prices keep rising at Noida Extension (Sec 1)’s 10-year trend of 6.9% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.66 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.7% a year. Noida Extension (Sec 1)’s 10-year trend is 2.8 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹70 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Noida Extension (Sec 1)?

About 3.1% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹18,000 a month on a home worth about ₹70 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 32, meaning the home costs 32 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Noida Extension (Sec 1)?

About ₹48,598 a month on a ₹56 L loan (20% down on a ₹70 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹33,515 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Noida Extension (Sec 1)?

About 6.9% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.5% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
