The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Andheri East

Andheri East is Mumbai's most important mid-market commercial and residential suburb, strategically located between the Western Express Highway, the Mumbai Metro Line 1, and Mumbai Airport. It is home to SEEPZ (software export zone), Marol IT Park, and a large number of corporate offices, making it the hub for mid-to-senior IT professionals who want central Mumbai access without South Mumbai prices.

  • SEEPZ & Marol IT Park hub
  • Airport proximity (2 km)
  • Metro Line 1 & 7 served
  • Mid-premium corporate belt
  • Strong rental demand

Major employers nearby: SEEPZ (Software Technology Park), Marol IT Park tenants, Reliance Corporate Office, HDFC Life, Atos India.

Getting around

  • Metro: Andheri Station (Metro Line 1 — Blue Line) — operational; Western Express Highway Station (Line 1); Gundavali Station (Line 7)
  • Highway: Western Express Highway — adjacent; Mumbai Airport — 2 km; Eastern Express Highway — 5 km via JVLR
  • Peak traffic: Very heavy on Western Express Highway and Andheri Station area 8–10:30 am and 6–9 pm; among Mumbai's busiest suburban nodes
  • Congestion: Very High

Schools

  • JBCN International School Oshiwara (IB/IGCSE) — Top 40 IB School globally (Education Advisers 2025); Top IB School in World 2024 & 2025
  • Ryan International School Andheri (CBSE) — Ryan chain campus; large school serving Andheri families
  • ST. Joseph's High School Andheri (ICSE) — Well-established ICSE school in Andheri West

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital (3 km), Seven Hills Hospital (2 km), Holy Spirit Hospital (2 km)
  • Malls: Infiniti Mall Andheri (2 km), Citi Mall (2 km), Oberoi Mall Goregaon (5 km)
  • Parks: Joggers Park Bandra (5 km), SEEPZ grounds, Juhu Beach (4 km)

Investment view

Andheri East offers strong 9–12% appreciation driven by airport proximity, SEEPZ, and metro connectivity. Rental yields at 3.5–4.5% with high occupancy from IT and airline professionals. Metro Line 1 has been a sustained price driver since 2014. Mid-entry price point makes it accessible compared to South Mumbai or BKC.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Andheri East?

Even if prices keep rising at Andheri East’s 10-year trend of 4.6% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹5.60 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.2% a year. Andheri East’s 10-year trend is 4.6 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.80 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Andheri East?

About 4.0% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹60,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.80 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 25, meaning the home costs 25 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Andheri East?

About ₹1,24,967 a month on a ₹1.44 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.80 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹72,467 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Andheri East?

About 4.6% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 3.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
