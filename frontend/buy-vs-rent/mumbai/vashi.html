The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Vashi

Vashi is Navi Mumbai's commercial capital and most mature suburb, home to Inorbit Mall, APMC market, and a large established residential population. It has the best connectivity in Navi Mumbai via Harbour Line suburban rail, easy access to Mumbai via MTHL sea bridge, and a growing IT commercial belt along the NH-4B corridor. Social infrastructure is excellent with top schools and hospitals.

  • Navi Mumbai commercial capital
  • Best suburban rail connectivity in NM
  • MTHL sea bridge to Mumbai
  • Inorbit Mall anchor
  • Mature established residential suburb

Major employers nearby: Vishwaroop IT Park (ICICI Lombard, Nomura, Alight Solutions), APMC market ecosystem, Navi Mumbai Municipal Corporation offices, Seawoods Grand Central corporate offices (adjacent).

Investment view

Vashi offers stable 8–10% appreciation with best-in-class Navi Mumbai connectivity. Rental yields at 4–4.5% are solid with strong demand from APMC traders and IT professionals. MTHL sea bridge has materially reduced travel time to Mumbai, acting as a sustained price catalyst. Best for stable long-term investors in Navi Mumbai.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Vashi?

Even if prices keep rising at Vashi’s 10-year trend of 6.9% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.31 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.4% a year. Vashi’s 10-year trend is 1.5 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.10 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Vashi?

About 4.3% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹39,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.10 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 24, meaning the home costs 24 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Vashi?

About ₹76,368 a month on a ₹88 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.10 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹41,952 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Vashi?

About 6.9% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.2% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
