About Mira Road

Mira Road is a large, dense suburb just north of Dahisar, inside the Mira-Bhayandar municipal area. It is one of the most affordable places to live with a Western Railway connection to Mumbai, which has made it hugely popular with families and first-time buyers. Metro Line 9 is being built to link it to Dahisar.

  • Affordable Western line suburb
  • Dense apartment market
  • Metro Line 9 (upcoming)
  • Shopping & markets
  • Close to Borivali

Major employers nearby: Offices in Andheri, Goregaon & Malad (by train), Local businesses & trade, Thane (via Ghodbunder Road).

Getting around

  • Metro: Metro Line 9 (Dahisar East–Mira Bhayandar) under construction; Mira Road station on the Western Railway
  • Highway: Western Express Highway (NH-48) ~2 km; Ghodbunder Road to Thane
  • Peak traffic: The Dahisar check naka and Western Express Highway entry are heavily congested in peaks
  • Congestion: High

Schools

  • Schools in Mira Road (several CBSE/ICSE/SSC) (Various) — Large choice of local schools
  • Schools in Borivali & Dahisar (Various) — More options ~6–8 km south

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Wockhardt Hospital, Mira Road, Bhaktivedanta Hospital
  • Malls: Thakur Mall, Mira Road, Retail along Mira-Bhayandar Road
  • Parks: Sanjay Gandhi National Park (~6 km), Local gardens

Investment view

Mira Road is a high-demand, budget-friendly market where rent is always easy to find. Metro Line 9 should improve the commute. 2BHK rents run about ₹20,000–24,000/mo. Good for families who need the Western line at a lower price.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Mira Road?

Even if prices keep rising at Mira Road’s 10-year trend of 7.4% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.37 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.4% a year. Mira Road’s 10-year trend is 2.0 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹80 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Mira Road?

About 3.3% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹22,000 a month on a home worth about ₹80 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 30, meaning the home costs 30 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Mira Road?

About ₹55,541 a month on a ₹64 L loan (20% down on a ₹80 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹36,874 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Mira Road?

About 7.4% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
