The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Lower Parel

Lower Parel is Mumbai's most dramatic urban transformation — old textile mill lands redeveloped into a premium commercial and residential district housing the highest concentration of luxury malls, media companies, and corporate offices between South Mumbai and BKC. It is the HQ of Hindustan Unilever, and home to offices of Google, McKinsey, and dozens of financial firms. Residential supply is very limited and ultra-premium.

  • Mill land redevelopment district
  • HUL & Google India HQ zone
  • Phoenix Palladium luxury retail
  • Ultra-premium limited residential
  • Financial and media company hub

Major employers nearby: Hindustan Unilever HQ, Google India (One BKC — nearby), McKinsey & Company India, STAR India HQ, Lodha commercial tenants (World One).

Investment view

Lower Parel is ultra-premium capital preservation at 8–10% appreciation. Residential units are among Mumbai's priciest and rental yields are moderate at 3–3.5% but tenant quality is exceptional. Aqua Line metro has cemented its connectivity advantage. Best for HNI investors seeking South Mumbai adjacency at a relative discount.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Lower Parel?

Even if prices keep rising at Lower Parel’s 10-year trend of 7.4% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹6.08 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.7% a year. Lower Parel’s 10-year trend is 2.2 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹3.00 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Lower Parel?

About 3.2% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹81,000 a month on a home worth about ₹3.00 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 31, meaning the home costs 31 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Lower Parel?

About ₹2,08,278 a month on a ₹2.40 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹3.00 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹1,39,778 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Lower Parel?

About 7.4% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
