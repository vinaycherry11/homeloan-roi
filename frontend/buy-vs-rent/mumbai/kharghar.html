The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Kharghar

Kharghar is a well-planned sector in Navi Mumbai developed by CIDCO, offering the best quality-of-life in the MMR at a fraction of Mumbai prices. It has wide roads, golf courses, and excellent schools. The opening of Navi Mumbai International Airport (NMIA) in December 2025 and the approaching Metro Line 8 have triggered 24–27% YoY price appreciation, making it one of India's fastest-appreciating residential markets.

  • CIDCO planned township
  • Navi Mumbai International Airport proximity
  • Golf course & sector parks
  • Metro Line 8 pipeline
  • 24–27% YoY appreciation

Major employers nearby: Reliance Corporate Park (nearby Ghansoli), TCS Navi Mumbai campus, DY Patil University ecosystem, Belapur CBD offices (7 km).

Investment view

Kharghar is seeing 24–27% YoY appreciation — among India's highest — driven by the NMIA airport opening (Dec 2025) and MTHL sea bridge access. Entry prices are still 40–60% below comparable Mumbai localities. Rental yields at 4–5%. Ideal for medium-term investors; the airport catalyst is still in early innings.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Kharghar?

Even if prices keep rising at Kharghar’s 10-year trend of 6.9% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹71.5 L richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.2% a year. Kharghar’s 10-year trend is 1.3 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹75 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Kharghar?

About 4.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹28,000 a month on a home worth about ₹75 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 22, meaning the home costs 22 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Kharghar?

About ₹52,069 a month on a ₹60 L loan (20% down on a ₹75 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹27,194 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Kharghar?

About 6.9% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.2% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
