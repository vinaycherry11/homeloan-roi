The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Bandra

Bandra is Mumbai's best-known suburb: sea-facing promenades, old bungalows, cafés, film stars and some of the city's most expensive homes. It is next to BKC and connected by the Bandra–Worli Sea Link. Supply comes mostly from redevelopment of older buildings.

  • Premium suburb
  • Next to BKC
  • Sea Link access
  • Cafés & nightlife
  • Heritage & sea front

Major employers nearby: BKC offices, Film & media industry, Consulates & corporate HQs.

  • Highway: Bandra–Worli Sea Link; Western Express Highway; coastal road links

Investment view

Bandra is a trophy market: prices are very high and yields low, so buyers are paying for the address and long-term scarcity. 2BHK rents run about ₹90,000–1,10,000/mo. Best for buyers for whom living here is the point.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Bandra?

Even if prices keep rising at Bandra’s 10-year trend of 4.6% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹13.63 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.8% a year. Bandra’s 10-year trend is 5.2 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹3.55 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Bandra?

About 3.4% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹1,00,000 a month on a home worth about ₹3.55 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 30, meaning the home costs 30 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Bandra?

About ₹2,46,462 a month on a ₹2.84 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹3.55 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹1,61,253 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Bandra?

About 4.6% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 3.2% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
