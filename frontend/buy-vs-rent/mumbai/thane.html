The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Thane

Thane West is Mumbai Metropolitan Region's fastest-growing suburban city, offering significantly more space at lower prices than Mumbai city while connecting via fast suburban rail to CST and Andheri in under 30 minutes. It is a self-contained city with excellent social infrastructure, multiple lakes, large residential townships, and a growing commercial belt. Upcoming Metro Line 4 and the Thane Ring Metro will further transform connectivity.

  • MMR's fastest-growing suburban city
  • Suburban rail to Mumbai in 30 min
  • Multiple lakes & green cover
  • Large township residential stock
  • Metro Line 4 & Ring Metro pipeline

Major employers nearby: Majiwada IT Park, Wagle Estate industrial & IT belt, Lodha commercial developments, Viviana Mall commercial ecosystem, Thane Municipal Corporation offices.

Getting around

  • Metro: Metro Line 4 (Wadala–Kasarvadavali) under construction; Thane Integral Ring Metro (29 km, 22 stations) approved Aug 2024
  • Highway: Eastern Express Highway — adjacent; Mumbai–Nashik Highway (NH-160) — 3 km; Ghodbunder Road (to Western suburbs) — 1 km
  • Peak traffic: Heavy on Ghodbunder Road and Thane station area 8–10 am and 6:30–9 pm; Eastern Express Highway from LBS Marg also congested
  • Congestion: High

Investment view

Thane West offers strong 10–14% appreciation at prices 30–40% below comparable Mumbai localities. Rental yields at 4–5% are among MMR's best outside Navi Mumbai. Metro Line 4 and Ring Metro approvals are major value catalysts. Best affordability–appreciation balance in the Mumbai region for mid-budget investors.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Thane?

Even if prices keep rising at Thane’s 10-year trend of 5.1% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹3.10 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.8% a year. Thane’s 10-year trend is 3.7 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.25 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Thane?

About 4.4% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹46,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.25 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 23, meaning the home costs 23 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Thane?

About ₹86,782 a month on a ₹1.00 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.25 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹45,991 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Thane?

About 5.1% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 3.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
