The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Andheri West

Andheri West is one of Mumbai's busiest and most connected suburbs, home to Lokhandwala, Versova, Four Bungalows and the film and TV industry. It is served by Metro Line 1 and Metro Line 2A, the Western line and the airport is close. Demand comes from professionals, media workers and families.

  • Metro Lines 1 & 2A
  • Lokhandwala & Versova
  • Media & entertainment hub
  • Close to airport
  • Restaurants & nightlife

Major employers nearby: Film & TV studios, Offices in Andheri East (SEEPZ, MIDC), BKC (~12 km).

Getting around

  • Metro: Metro Line 1: DN Nagar, Azad Nagar, Andheri; Metro Line 2A: Andheri West, DN Nagar
  • Highway: Western Express Highway ~3 km; JVLR & SV Road
  • Peak traffic: JP Road, SV Road and Link Road are congested much of the day
  • Congestion: High

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital, Criticare & local hospitals
  • Malls: Infiniti Mall, Andheri, Lokhandwala market
  • Parks: Versova beach, Local gardens

Investment view

Andheri West rents easily and holds value, but prices are already high so yields are modest. 2BHK rents run about ₹55,000–65,000/mo. A strong choice for buyers who want a central, well-connected Western suburb.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Andheri West?

Even if prices keep rising at Andheri West’s 10-year trend of 6.0% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹5.31 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.4% a year. Andheri West’s 10-year trend is 3.4 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹2.00 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Andheri West?

About 3.6% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹60,000 a month on a home worth about ₹2.00 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 28, meaning the home costs 28 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Andheri West?

About ₹1,38,852 a month on a ₹1.60 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹2.00 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹87,185 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Andheri West?

About 6.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.4% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
