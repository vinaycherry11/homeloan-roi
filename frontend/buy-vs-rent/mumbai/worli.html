The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Worli

Worli is a central Mumbai sea-front district that has changed from mill land into one of India's most expensive luxury housing markets, with high-rise towers, corporate offices and the Bandra–Worli Sea Link. The Aqua Line (Metro 3) and the Coastal Road have improved access further.

  • Luxury high-rises
  • Sea Link & Coastal Road
  • Aqua Line (Metro 3)
  • Corporate offices
  • Sea-facing homes

Major employers nearby: Corporate offices in Worli & Lower Parel, BKC (~6 km), Nariman Point (via Coastal Road).

Getting around

  • Metro: Aqua Line (Metro 3): Worli and Siddhivinayak stations; Lower Parel station (Western line)
  • Highway: Bandra–Worli Sea Link; Mumbai Coastal Road; Eastern Freeway nearby
  • Peak traffic: Annie Besant Road and the Sea Link approach are congested at peaks
  • Congestion: High

Schools

  • Schools in Worli & Prabhadevi (Various) — Several premium schools nearby

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Hospitals in Worli & Prabhadevi, Major hospitals in Mahalaxmi & Parel (~3 km)
  • Malls: Phoenix Palladium, Lower Parel (~2 km)
  • Parks: Worli Sea Face promenade, Worli Fort

Investment view

Worli is the top end of the Mumbai market. Rents are high in absolute terms but low relative to price, so the case for buying rests on wealth preservation and lifestyle. 2BHK rents run about ₹1,00,000–1,25,000/mo. Suits buyers who want a premium central address and do not need rental returns.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Worli?

Even if prices keep rising at Worli’s 10-year trend of 6.0% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹12.76 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.8% a year. Worli’s 10-year trend is 3.8 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹4.10 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Worli?

About 3.3% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹1,12,000 a month on a home worth about ₹4.10 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 31, meaning the home costs 31 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Worli?

About ₹2,84,646 a month on a ₹3.28 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹4.10 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹1,89,729 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Worli?

About 6.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 3.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
