The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Mulund

Mulund is the last suburb on the Central line before Thane, with a reputation for clean, planned streets and a strong community feel. Large new towers along LBS Marg and Mulund West sit next to older housing societies. Metro Line 4 (Wadala–Kasarvadavali) is being built through the area.

  • Central line suburb
  • Metro Line 4 (upcoming)
  • Close to Thane
  • Planned neighbourhoods
  • Gated towers

Major employers nearby: Offices in Thane & Airoli, Powai (~10 km), Mulund industrial estates.

Getting around

  • Metro: Metro Line 4 along LBS Marg (under construction); Mulund station on the Central line
  • Highway: Eastern Express Highway ~2 km; Mulund–Airoli Bridge to Navi Mumbai
  • Peak traffic: LBS Marg and the Mulund check naka are congested at peak hours
  • Congestion: Moderate

Schools

  • Schools in Mulund East & West (Various) — Many established CBSE, ICSE and SSC schools

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Fortis Hospital, Mulund, Apex Hospital, Mulund
  • Malls: R Mall, Mulund, Viviana Mall, Thane (~6 km)
  • Parks: Sanjay Gandhi National Park (Mulund side), Local gardens

Investment view

Mulund is a steady, family-oriented market. Price growth has been moderate, but demand is consistent and Metro Line 4 should help. 2BHK rents run about ₹35,000–42,000/mo. A sound self-use choice for families working in the eastern suburbs or Thane.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Mulund?

Even if prices keep rising at Mulund’s 10-year trend of 6.0% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹3.44 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.4% a year. Mulund’s 10-year trend is 3.4 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.30 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Mulund?

About 3.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹38,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.30 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 29, meaning the home costs 29 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Mulund?

About ₹90,254 a month on a ₹1.04 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.30 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹57,670 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Mulund?

About 6.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
