The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Ambawadi

Ambawadi is a central Ahmedabad area west of the river, between Paldi and Satellite. It has educational institutions, hospitals, shops and established housing, with good access to the city centre.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Ambawadi?

Even if prices keep rising at Ambawadi’s 10-year trend of 7.0% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹2.45 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.8% a year. Ambawadi’s 10-year trend is 2.8 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.00 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Ambawadi?

About 3.1% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹26,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.00 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 32, meaning the home costs 32 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Ambawadi?

About ₹69,426 a month on a ₹80 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.00 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹47,593 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Ambawadi?

About 7.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.7% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
