The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About GIFT City

GIFT City (Gujarat International Finance Tec-City) is India's first operational smart city and International Financial Services Centre (IFSC), located on the Ahmedabad-Gandhinagar Expressway, 15 km north of Ahmedabad. It hosts 250+ financial firms and global MNCs including Oracle, Bank of America, and TCS, governed by IFSCA under a special regulatory framework. The Violet Line metro spur (GNLU–GIFT City, opened Sept 2024) provides direct metro access. Property prices surged 61.5% in 3 years — India's highest appreciation rate for any new township.

  • Oracle, Bank of America, TCS, HDFC, SBI

Investment view

GIFT City is Ahmedabad's highest-conviction long-term bet — 61.5% rise in 3 years, 10.5% last year, pricing at ₹8,500–12,800/sqft. Violet Line metro is already priced in; Yellow Line Gandhinagar connectivity adds further appeal. Best for investors targeting the 5–10 year India financial infrastructure cycle.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in GIFT City?

If prices keep rising at GIFT City’s 10-year trend of 10.5% a year, buying leaves you about ₹1.43 Cr richer after 20 years than renting and investing the difference. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.5% a year. GIFT City’s 10-year trend is 1.0 points above that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.20 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in GIFT City?

About 3.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹35,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.20 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 29, meaning the home costs 29 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in GIFT City?

About ₹83,311 a month on a ₹96 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.20 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹53,311 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in GIFT City?

About 10.5% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.5% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
