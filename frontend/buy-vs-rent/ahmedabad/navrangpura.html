The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Navrangpura

Satellite and Navrangpura form Ahmedabad's premium central-west belt — mature, densely developed neighbourhoods home to IIM Ahmedabad, Gujarat University, Law Garden, and the city's corporate and professional class. Satellite averages ₹8,200–8,500/sqft with 10.4% appreciation last year. The Blue Line metro passes through Navrangpura (Shreyas and Commerce Six Roads stations), making it one of the best-connected central zones. CG Road is Ahmedabad's finest dining and retail street.

  • IIM Ahmedabad adjacency (4 km)
  • Gujarat University & top colleges in Navrangpura
  • Blue Line metro — Shreyas & Commerce Six Roads stations
  • Law Garden & CG Road retail/dining
  • Premium central address with no new land supply

Major employers nearby: IIM Ahmedabad campus (faculty, admin), Gujarat High Court (Navrangpura) — 3 km, CG Road commercial offices, Gujarat University staff, Insurance & banking offices along Navrangpura Road.

Investment view

Satellite/Navrangpura delivers 10–12% annual appreciation backed by IIM Ahmedabad premium, Blue Line metro, and near-zero new land supply. Prices at ₹7,500–8,500/sqft are strong but still 50–60% below Mumbai equivalents. Rental yields at 3.5–4.5% from students, young professionals, and academics. Best for buyers wanting a premium central Ahmedabad address with institutional prestige.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Navrangpura?

Even if prices keep rising at Navrangpura’s 10-year trend of 7.0% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.39 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.9% a year. Navrangpura’s 10-year trend is 1.9 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹90 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Navrangpura?

About 4.0% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹30,000 a month on a home worth about ₹90 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 25, meaning the home costs 25 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Navrangpura?

About ₹62,483 a month on a ₹72 L loan (20% down on a ₹90 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹36,233 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Navrangpura?

About 7.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.7% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
