The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. About SG Highway (North)

Sarkhej-Gandhinagar Highway (SG Highway) is Ahmedabad's commercial and residential spine — a 30-km arterial that has transformed into the city's most coveted mixed-use corridor. Lined with Grade A IT parks, retail malls, and premium residences, it is home to the largest concentration of MNC offices in Gujarat. The Blue Line metro (Thaltej Gam–Vastral Gam, operational Dec 2024) and a dense BRTS network form the transit backbone. SG Highway is the first address Ahmedabad buyers look at for work-live proximity.

  • Ahmedabad's prime commercial-residential corridor
  • Largest MNC office concentration in Gujarat
  • Blue Line metro — Thaltej end (Dec 2024)
  • 15–22% annual appreciation zone
  • Parshwanath & Pinnacle business hubs

Major employers nearby: TCS (Tata Consultancy Services), Wipro Technologies, HCL Technologies, Capgemini, Parshwanath Business Park, Pinnacle Business Park, KPMG India.

Getting around

  • Metro: Blue Line (East-West): Thaltej Gam (terminus) and Thaltej stations — opened Dec 2024; 12.5 km Bopal-Sanathal extension planned. BRTS: frequent services along entire SG Highway corridor
  • Highway: 132 Feet Ring Road — intersects at multiple points; Sarkhej-Gandhinagar NH-147 — forms the corridor; Airport via NH-48 — 12 km
  • Peak traffic: Heavy on SG Highway main road during 8:30–10:30 am and 6–9 pm; Bopal Junction and Prahlad Nagar junction are worst spots; BRTS lanes improve bus flow
  • Congestion: High

Investment view

SG Highway is Ahmedabad's strongest appreciation story — 15–22% per year in premium pockets, 25–35% cumulative over 5 years. Prices at ₹8,000–10,500/sqft are still far below comparable Bangalore or Hyderabad IT corridors. Rental yields at 4–5% from IT professionals make it a strong income play. Blue Line metro at Thaltej unlocks further capital gain. Best all-round Ahmedabad investment for 2024–2027.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in SG Highway (North)?

At SG Highway (North)’s 10-year price trend of 8.0% a year, the two choices end up within ₹6.7 L of each other after 20 years (renting slightly ahead). Small changes in price growth, rent or your loan rate decide it. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.1% a year. SG Highway (North)’s 10-year trend is 0.1 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.05 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in SG Highway (North)?

About 4.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹39,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.05 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 22, meaning the home costs 22 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in SG Highway (North)?

About ₹72,897 a month on a ₹84 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.05 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹38,272 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in SG Highway (North)?

About 8.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.5% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
