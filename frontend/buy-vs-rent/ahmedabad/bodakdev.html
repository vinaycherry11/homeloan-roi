The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Bodakdev

Bodakdev is west Ahmedabad's most upscale residential enclave — a leafy, low-density suburb between SG Highway and Satellite with luxury villa row houses, premium high-rises, and the headquarters of Zydus Lifesciences. Home to Ahmedabad International School (IB/IGCSE) and Zydus School for Excellence, it attracts senior executives and Gujarat's business families. The Blue Line metro at Thaltej Gam (2 km, opened Dec 2024) has driven 10–12% price appreciation since opening.

Investment view

Bodakdev is Ahmedabad's prestige capital preservation play — 10–12% steady appreciation, very limited new supply, and a tenant base of senior executives and business families. Prices at ₹12,000–18,000/sqft for premium units are Ahmedabad's highest outside GIFT City. Rental yields at 3–3.5% are modest but tenant quality is exceptional. Best for HNI buyers seeking a prestigious low-density Ahmedabad address.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Bodakdev?

Even if prices keep rising at Bodakdev’s 10-year trend of 7.0% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹3.95 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.7% a year. Bodakdev’s 10-year trend is 2.7 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.70 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Bodakdev?

About 3.2% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹46,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.70 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 31, meaning the home costs 31 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Bodakdev?

About ₹1,18,024 a month on a ₹1.36 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.70 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹79,107 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Bodakdev?

About 7.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.7% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
