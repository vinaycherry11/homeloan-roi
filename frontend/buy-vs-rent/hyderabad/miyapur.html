The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Miyapur

Miyapur is a large, affordable western suburb that serves as a transit gateway with Metro Phase 1 terminus at one end. It has high absorption of budget 2BHK apartments and attracts first-time buyers and young IT workers. Infrastructure has improved significantly since metro arrival and the area benefits from proximity to the Chandanagar IT belt.

  • Metro Phase 1 terminus
  • Affordable entry point
  • Large inventory
  • Chandanagar IT spillover
  • Budget-friendly suburb

Major employers nearby: Mindspace Business Park (8 km), DLF Cybercity (10 km), Hitech City offices (12 km).

Getting around

  • Peak traffic: Heavy traffic 8–10 am and 6:30–9 pm near Miyapur Metro and Allwyn X-Roads

Investment view

Miyapur is an affordable growth zone with metro-boosted appreciation of 7–8% p.a. and decent gross rental yields of about 4–4.5%. Best suited for first-time buyers and investors targeting budget tenants in the IT segment.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Miyapur?

Even if prices keep rising at Miyapur’s 10-year trend of 7.8% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹40.2 L richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.5% a year. Miyapur’s 10-year trend is 0.7 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹70 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Miyapur?

About 4.3% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹25,000 a month on a home worth about ₹70 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 23, meaning the home costs 23 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Miyapur?

About ₹48,598 a month on a ₹56 L loan (20% down on a ₹70 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹26,515 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Miyapur?

About 7.8% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.0% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
