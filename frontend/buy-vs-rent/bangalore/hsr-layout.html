The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About HSR Layout

HSR Layout (Hosur-Sarjapur Road Layout) has transformed from a quiet BDA township into Bangalore's secondary startup and tech hub. It sits at the intersection of the Outer Ring Road IT belt and Koramangala, making it a top address for mid-to-senior tech professionals who want proximity to work without the density of Koramangala or Indiranagar. Residential stock ranges from old BDA plots to new premium apartments.

  • ORR-Koramangala crossroads
  • Secondary startup hub
  • Mid-senior IT professional zone
  • Well-planned BDA layout
  • Growing café & co-working culture

Major employers nearby: Flipkart office, Samsung R&D (ORR), Oracle India (ORR), Texas Instruments (Bannerghatta Road), Tesco Technology Centre.

Getting around

  • Metro: Agara (HSR) station on Purple Line Phase 2 under construction; Silk Board station (Purple Line) — 2 km currently
  • Highway: Outer Ring Road — 1 km; Hosur Road (NH-44) via Silk Board — 3 km
  • Peak traffic: Moderate to high on BDA Complex Road and Agara Junction 8:30–10 am and 6:30–9 pm; ORR onramp is the key bottleneck
  • Congestion: High

Schools

  • National Public School HSR Layout (CBSE) — Well-regarded NPS chain school in the locality
  • Delhi Public School South Bangalore (CBSE) — Large DPS campus serving south Bangalore
  • Harvest International School (IGCSE) — IGCSE school in HSR serving expat families

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Fortis Hospital Bannerghatta Road (4 km), Apollo Spectra Koramangala (3 km), Narayana Health City (8 km)
  • Malls: Forum Mall Koramangala (3 km), Market Square Mall (5 km), Soul Space Arena (4 km)
  • Parks: Agara Lake (1 km), Kudlu Gate Park (2 km)

Investment view

HSR Layout delivers 9–11% appreciation with strong rental demand from ORR IT corridor workers. Rental yields at 4.5–5% make it one of Bangalore's better balanced investment localities. Metro Phase 2 construction at Agara is a near-term value catalyst expected to lift prices upon completion.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in HSR Layout?

If prices keep rising at HSR Layout’s 10-year trend of 9.3% a year, buying leaves you about ₹1.48 Cr richer after 20 years than renting and investing the difference. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.0% a year. HSR Layout’s 10-year trend is 1.3 points above that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.25 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in HSR Layout?

About 4.4% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹46,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.25 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 23, meaning the home costs 23 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in HSR Layout?

About ₹86,782 a month on a ₹1.00 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.25 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹45,991 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in HSR Layout?

About 9.3% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
