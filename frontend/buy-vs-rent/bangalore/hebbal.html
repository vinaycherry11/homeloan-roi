The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. About Hebbal

Hebbal is north Bangalore's premier residential and commercial gateway, anchored by Manyata Tech Park — one of Bangalore's largest IT office parks. It sits at the confluence of NH-44 (Bengaluru–Delhi highway) and Bellary Road, offering superb airport access (15 minutes). Residential demand is driven by Manyata Tech Park employees and families seeking north Bangalore's relatively lower density and cleaner air.

  • Manyata Tech Park gateway
  • Airport highway corridor
  • North Bangalore's prime address
  • ORR access
  • Emerging luxury residential

Major employers nearby: Manyata Tech Park tenants (IBM, Cognizant, Philips, Accenture, Target), Embassy Manyata Business Park, Kirloskar Business Park (2 km).

Investment view

Hebbal delivers strong 10–12% appreciation backed by Manyata Tech Park's massive employment base. Rental yields at 4.5–5% are solid. Airport corridor positioning makes it attractive for executives and expats. Proposed Airport Metro Line will be a major price catalyst when confirmed and built.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Hebbal?

If prices keep rising at Hebbal’s 10-year trend of 8.8% a year, buying leaves you about ₹94.7 L richer after 20 years than renting and investing the difference. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 7.9% a year. Hebbal’s 10-year trend is 0.9 points above that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.20 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Hebbal?

About 4.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹45,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.20 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 22, meaning the home costs 22 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Hebbal?

About ₹83,311 a month on a ₹96 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.20 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹43,311 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Hebbal?

About 8.8% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
