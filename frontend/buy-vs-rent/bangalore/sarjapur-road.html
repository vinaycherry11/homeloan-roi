The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Sarjapur Road

Sarjapur Road has emerged as one of Bangalore's hottest residential corridors, connecting the Outer Ring Road IT belt to Electronic City and Whitefield. It benefits from a massive employment catchment — thousands of IT workers from Wipro Sarjapur Campus, Infosys, and Accenture campuses commute from here. Prices appreciated ~63% between 2021 and 2024, and residential launches have been dominated by premium large-format gated communities.

  • ORR-to-Electronic City connector
  • Premium gated community belt
  • Rapid 2021–2024 appreciation
  • Large township projects
  • Startup & IT professional catchment

Major employers nearby: Wipro Sarjapur Campus, Infosys Sarjapur Campus, Accenture Sarjapur, RGA Tech Park tenants, Cessna Business Park tenants.

Getting around

  • Metro: No metro currently; Phase 3A Silk Board–Sarjapur corridor proposed but not yet sanctioned
  • Highway: Outer Ring Road — 1.5 km at Carmelaram; NH-44 via Electronic City — 8 km
  • Peak traffic: Very congested on Sarjapur Main Road and Carmelaram junction 8–10 am and 6:30–9 pm; bottleneck at ORR-Sarjapur Road intersection
  • Congestion: Very High

Schools

  • Inventure Academy (IB/IGCSE/CBSE) — One of Bangalore's top international schools; IB and Cambridge
  • Oakridge International School (Sarjapur) (IB/CBSE) — Premium IB school on Sarjapur Road
  • Candor International School (IB) — Full IB continuum school serving the Sarjapur corridor

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Columbia Asia Hospital Sarjapur (3 km), Motherhood Hospital Sarjapur (2 km), Narayana Health City (10 km)
  • Malls: Market Square Mall Sarjapur (2 km), Nexus Whitefield (10 km), Forum Mall Koramangala (12 km)
  • Parks: Sarjapur Lake (local), Carmelaram Park (2 km)

Investment view

Sarjapur Road delivered ~63% price appreciation from 2021–2024 and continues at 10–12% p.a. as new township completions absorb strong demand. No metro yet is the main risk — traffic is severe. Rental yields at 4.5–5%. Best suited for 5–7 year hold investors banking on metro announcement upside.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Sarjapur Road?

If prices keep rising at Sarjapur Road’s 10-year trend of 10.3% a year, buying leaves you about ₹2.75 Cr richer after 20 years than renting and investing the difference. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 7.6% a year. Sarjapur Road’s 10-year trend is 2.7 points above that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.05 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Sarjapur Road?

About 4.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹39,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.05 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 22, meaning the home costs 22 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Sarjapur Road?

About ₹72,897 a month on a ₹84 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.05 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹38,272 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Sarjapur Road?

About 10.3% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 6.5% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
