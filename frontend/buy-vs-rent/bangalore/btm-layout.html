About BTM Layout

BTM Layout (Bangalore–Tumkur–Mysore Layout) is an affordable, dense residential suburb in south Bangalore popular with young IT professionals and students. It offers budget apartments at prices well below Koramangala or HSR Layout while sharing similar proximity to the Outer Ring Road IT belt. Koramangala and HSR Layout are within 3 km, making it a popular first-home or rental investment zone.

  • Affordable south Bangalore
  • Young IT professional hub
  • ORR & Koramangala proximity
  • High rental demand
  • Budget-friendly apartments

Major employers nearby: ORR IT belt (Wipro, Flipkart, Samsung — 3 km), Koramangala startup offices (3 km), Electronic City (12 km), NICE Road tech parks (5 km).

Getting around

  • Metro: BTM Layout Station proposed on Phase 3 Kalena Agrahara corridor; Silk Board (Purple Line) — 1.5 km currently
  • Highway: Outer Ring Road via Silk Board — 2 km; Hosur Road (NH-44) — 3 km
  • Peak traffic: Heavy at BTM Layout 2nd Stage junction and Silk Board underpass approach 8:30–10 am and 6:30–9 pm
  • Congestion: High

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Fortis Hospital Bannerghatta Road (4 km), Apollo Spectra Koramangala (3 km), Sparsh Hospital (3 km)
  • Malls: Forum Mall Koramangala (3 km), Market Square Mall (5 km), Vega City Mall (5 km)
  • Parks: Madiwala Lake (2 km), Agara Lake (2 km)

Investment view

BTM Layout is primarily a rental income play with gross yields of about 4–4.5% driven by high demand from IT and startup workers. Capital appreciation at 7–8% p.a. is steady but moderate. Very affordable entry prices make it accessible to first-time investors. Silk Board metro proximity is the key value anchor.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in BTM Layout?

At BTM Layout’s 10-year price trend of 8.2% a year, the two choices end up within ₹33.2 L of each other after 20 years (renting slightly ahead). Small changes in price growth, rent or your loan rate decide it. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.5% a year. BTM Layout’s 10-year trend is 0.3 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.15 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in BTM Layout?

About 4.3% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹41,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.15 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 23, meaning the home costs 23 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in BTM Layout?

About ₹79,840 a month on a ₹92 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.15 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹43,631 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in BTM Layout?

About 8.2% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.9% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
