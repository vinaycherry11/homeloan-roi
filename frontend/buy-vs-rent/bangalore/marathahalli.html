The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Marathahalli

Marathahalli is a densely populated mid-market residential and commercial suburb on Bangalore's eastern Outer Ring Road, serving as one of the primary bedroom communities for the vast ORR and Whitefield IT belt. It has a large retail and service economy, affordable to mid-range apartment stock, and high rental demand from IT professionals at various salary levels. Connectivity to both Whitefield and Koramangala via ORR is a structural advantage.

  • ORR eastern residential hub
  • High IT worker density
  • Affordable-to-mid apartments
  • Retail & service hub
  • KR Puram rail & Purple Line access

Major employers nearby: Bagmane Tech Park (3 km), RMZ Ecoworld (4 km), International Tech Park Whitefield (6 km), Wipro Sarjapur Campus (8 km), Embassy Tech Village (5 km).

Getting around

  • Metro: KR Puram Metro Station (Purple Line) — 3 km; Marathahalli station proposed in Phase 2 ORR corridor
  • Highway: Outer Ring Road — adjacent; Old Airport Road — 1 km
  • Peak traffic: Very heavy congestion on ORR and Marathahalli Bridge 8–10 am and 6:30–9 pm; one of Bangalore's most congested junctions
  • Congestion: Very High

  • Malls: Phoenix Marketcity (4 km), Innovative Multiplex (2 km), VR Bengaluru (5 km)
  • Parks: Krishnarajapura Lake (3 km), Bagmane Tech Park lake area

Investment view

Marathahalli offers solid 8–10% appreciation and strong gross rental yields of about 4–4.5% at mid-range entry prices — making it one of Bangalore's better rental income localities. Metro Phase 2 extension on the ORR will be a significant upside trigger. Best for investors targeting mid-income IT tenants.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Marathahalli?

If prices keep rising at Marathahalli’s 10-year trend of 11.0% a year, buying leaves you about ₹3.34 Cr richer after 20 years than renting and investing the difference. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 7.9% a year. Marathahalli’s 10-year trend is 3.1 points above that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.00 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Marathahalli?

About 4.2% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹35,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.00 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 24, meaning the home costs 24 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Marathahalli?

About ₹69,426 a month on a ₹80 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.00 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹38,593 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Marathahalli?

About 11.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 6.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
