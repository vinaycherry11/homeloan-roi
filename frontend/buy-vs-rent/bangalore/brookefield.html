About Brookefield

Brookefield (also written Brookfield) is a mid-premium residential locality in east Bangalore adjacent to Whitefield's ITPB, offering more affordable prices than Whitefield while sharing its IT catchment. It has good access to the Purple Line metro via KR Puram and a mix of independent houses, gated communities, and apartment blocks. Popular with mid-level IT professionals who want proximity to the Whitefield belt without peak Whitefield prices.

  • Whitefield fringe at lower prices
  • ITPB & Bagmane Tech Park proximity
  • Purple Line metro access via KR Puram
  • Mix of old houses & new apartments
  • Mid-premium IT professional zone

Major employers nearby: ITPB tenants (Oracle, IBM, TCS) — 1 km, Bagmane Tech Park (2 km), Kalyani Tech Park (2 km), Manyata Tech Park (15 km via ORR).

Investment view

Brookefield is an affordable entry into the Whitefield IT catchment at 8–10% appreciation. Rental yields at 4.5–5% with 2BHK rents of ₹22,000–38,000/mo. Purple Line metro at KR Puram and Hoodi has been a consistent price driver since 2023. Good value-for-money play in east Bangalore.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Brookefield?

At Brookefield’s 10-year price trend of 9.5% a year, the two choices end up within ₹28.1 L of each other after 20 years (buying slightly ahead). Small changes in price growth, rent or your loan rate decide it. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.2% a year. Brookefield’s 10-year trend is 0.2 points above that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.10 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Brookefield?

About 3.3% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹30,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.10 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 31, meaning the home costs 31 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Brookefield?

About ₹76,368 a month on a ₹88 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.10 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹50,952 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Brookefield?

About 9.5% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 6.3% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
