The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Jayanagar

Jayanagar is one of Bangalore's most prestigious and mature planned townships, developed in the 1960s and known for wide roads, lush tree cover, and a large middle-to-upper-class owner-occupied base. It has minimal new residential supply, a strong sense of community, excellent schools, and some of Bangalore's best hospitals. The Green Line metro now runs through Jayanagar, adding a new connectivity dimension to this classic address.

  • Old Bangalore prestige address
  • Green Line metro served
  • Very low new supply
  • Wide roads & tree cover
  • Elite schools & hospitals

Major employers nearby: Central Government offices (nearby), NIMHANS (3 km), Judicial establishments, Bannerghatta Road IT belt (6 km), Koramangala startup belt (4 km).

Getting around

  • Metro: Jayanagar Metro Station (Green Line) — operational; multiple nearby Green Line stations
  • Highway: Bannerghatta Road — 1 km; NICE Road — 4 km via Banashankari
  • Peak traffic: Moderate; 11th Main Road and South End Circle can slow 9–10 am; Jayanagar is notably less congested than most Bangalore areas
  • Congestion: Moderate

Schools

  • National Public School Jayanagar (NPS) (CBSE) — Among Bangalore's most sought-after schools; very competitive admissions
  • St. Joseph's College of Commerce (nearby) (Degree/PU) — Prestigious Jesuit institution, not school but major landmark
  • Vijaya High School (State/CBSE) — Historic school with strong alumni base across Jayanagar

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Jayadeva Institute of Cardiovascular Sciences (1 km), Apollo BGS Hospital (4 km), Sparsh Hospital (3 km)
  • Malls: Garuda Mall (3 km), Vega City Mall (4 km), Gopalan Innovation Mall (5 km)
  • Parks: Lalbagh Botanical Garden (2 km), Jayanagar 4th Block Park (local)

Investment view

Jayanagar is a premium capital-preservation play at 7.5–9% p.a. appreciation with very limited new supply. Rental yields are modest at 3.5–4% given high base prices, but tenant quality is premium. Green Line metro has added a new demand layer from younger professionals. Best for long-term hold investors.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Jayanagar?

Even if prices keep rising at Jayanagar’s 10-year trend of 7.5% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹2.08 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.1% a year. Jayanagar’s 10-year trend is 1.6 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.50 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Jayanagar?

About 3.8% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹47,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.50 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 27, meaning the home costs 27 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Jayanagar?

About ₹1,04,139 a month on a ₹1.20 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.50 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹63,389 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Jayanagar?

About 7.5% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.9% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
