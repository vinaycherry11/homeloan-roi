The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Indiranagar

Indiranagar is Bangalore's most coveted urban neighbourhood — a tree-lined, well-planned area popular with expats, senior professionals, and the creative class. It combines premium residential apartments and old independent houses with Bangalore's best restaurant and retail strip (100 Feet Road). Blue Line metro connectivity, low new supply, and high aspirational demand keep it as one of the city's top price-appreciation localities.

  • Premium urban lifestyle address
  • 100 Feet Road dining & retail
  • Purple Line metro connected
  • Expat favourite
  • Very low new residential supply

Major employers nearby: Numerous MNC offices on nearby ORR, Embassy Golf Links Business Park (4 km), Bagmane Tech Park (3 km), HAL Aerospace (2 km).

Getting around

  • Highway: Old Airport Road — 0.5 km; Outer Ring Road via Domlur — 3 km
  • Peak traffic: Moderate–high on 100 Feet Road 9–10:30 am and 7–9 pm; CMH Road can bottleneck near metro
  • Congestion: Moderate

Investment view

Indiranagar is Bangalore's scarcest residential micro-market — almost no new launches — making it a pure capital appreciation and prestige hold at 9–11% p.a. Rental yields at 3.5–4.5% are modest but tenant quality is exceptional. Best for long-term HNI or expat-targeting investors.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Indiranagar?

Even if prices keep rising at Indiranagar’s 10-year trend of 8.2% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹94.3 L richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.8% a year. Indiranagar’s 10-year trend is 0.6 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.70 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Indiranagar?

About 4.0% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹57,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.70 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 25, meaning the home costs 25 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Indiranagar?

About ₹1,18,024 a month on a ₹1.36 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.70 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹68,107 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Indiranagar?

About 8.2% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.9% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
