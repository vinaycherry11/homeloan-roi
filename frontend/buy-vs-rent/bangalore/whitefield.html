The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Whitefield

Whitefield is Bangalore's oldest and largest IT corridor, anchored by International Tech Park Bangalore (ITPB) and the EPIP Zone, and now fully connected to the city centre via the Purple Line metro (opened October 2023). Spanning over 1,000 global company campuses across multiple tech parks, it employs several hundred thousand IT professionals and commands strong residential demand year-round. The area has matured into a full township with malls, hospitals, and international schools.

  • Largest IT corridor in Bangalore
  • Purple Line metro connected
  • ITPB & EPIP Zone hub
  • International school belt
  • Premium gated communities

Major employers nearby: Infosys (285-acre campus), IBM, TCS, Wipro, SAP Labs India, Oracle.

Getting around

  • Metro: Whitefield (Kadugodi) Metro Station — Purple Line terminus — on-site; 12-station eastern extension operational since Oct 2023
  • Highway: Outer Ring Road (ORR) — 3 km via Marathahalli junction; NH-648 (Old Madras Road) — 4 km
  • Peak traffic: Heavy congestion on Whitefield Main Road and Hope Farm junction 8–10 am and 6–9 pm; Old Airport Road approach also bottlenecks
  • Congestion: High

Schools

  • TCIS Whitefield (The Cambridge International School) (CBSE/IB) — Rank #2 Emerging School Bangalore, TOI 2024; British Council award
  • NPS Whitefield (National Public School) (CBSE) — Part of the renowned NPS chain; strong academics
  • Indus International School (IB) — 40-acre campus, full IB continuum, 33+ nationalities

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Manipal Hospital Whitefield (1.5 km), Columbia Asia Hospital Whitefield (2 km), Narayana Multispeciality Hospital (3 km)
  • Malls: Phoenix Marketcity (3 km), VR Bengaluru (4 km), Nexus Whitefield (1 km)
  • Parks: ITPB lake & grounds, Channasandra Lake Park (2 km)

Investment view

Whitefield is among Bangalore's strongest investment micro-markets with 9–12% p.a. appreciation driven by metro connectivity, large tech campus demand, and limited new supply near ITPB. Rental yields are 4–5% with 2BHK rents at ₹30,000–55,000/mo. Metro access since Oct 2023 has structurally lifted prices.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Whitefield?

If prices keep rising at Whitefield’s 10-year trend of 9.7% a year, buying leaves you about ₹1.82 Cr richer after 20 years than renting and investing the difference. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 7.7% a year. Whitefield’s 10-year trend is 2.0 points above that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.00 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Whitefield?

About 4.4% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹37,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.00 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 23, meaning the home costs 23 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Whitefield?

About ₹69,426 a month on a ₹80 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.00 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹36,593 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Whitefield?

About 9.7% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 6.3% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
