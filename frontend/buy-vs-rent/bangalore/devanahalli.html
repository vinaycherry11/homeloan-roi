About Devanahalli

Devanahalli is the town next to Kempegowda International Airport on Bangalore's far north. Over the last decade it has turned from farmland into a belt of plotted layouts, villa projects and large apartment townships, pulled along by the airport, the Aerospace SEZ and the KIADB hardware and IT parks around it. Most residents commute to Hebbal, Manyata or the airport itself.

  • Airport-adjacent location
  • Aerospace & hardware SEZs
  • Plotted layouts & villas
  • Large townships
  • Devanahalli Fort heritage

Major employers nearby: Kempegowda International Airport (BIAL), Airlines & airport services, Aerospace SEZ units, Logistics & warehousing firms.

Getting around

  • Metro: No metro yet; the Airport Line (Phase 2B, Blue Line) via Hebbal is under construction
  • Highway: NH-44 (Bellary Road) runs through the town; Satellite Town Ring Road and STRR planned nearby
  • Peak traffic: Light inside Devanahalli; the run into the city along Bellary Road (NH-44) slows sharply past Yelahanka and at Hebbal in peak hours
  • Congestion: Low

Schools

  • Canadian International School (IB/IGCSE) — International school on the airport road belt
  • Delhi Public School, Bangalore North (CBSE) — Large CBSE campus off the airport road

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Akash Hospital (local), Hospitals at Yelahanka (~15 km)
  • Malls: Retail around the airport road, Malls at Yelahanka and Hebbal (~20–30 km)
  • Parks: Devanahalli Fort, Nandi Hills (~25 km)

Investment view

Devanahalli is a long-horizon bet on the airport and the planned business parks around it. Supply is large, so resale and rental demand can be thin in newer projects, and most demand today comes from airport and aviation staff. 2BHK rents run about ₹20,000–24,000/mo in completed townships. Buy here for growth you are willing to wait for, not for rent.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Devanahalli?

If prices keep rising at Devanahalli’s 10-year trend of 10.3% a year, buying leaves you about ₹85.1 L richer after 20 years than renting and investing the difference. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.4% a year. Devanahalli’s 10-year trend is 0.9 points above that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹80 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Devanahalli?

About 3.3% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹22,000 a month on a home worth about ₹80 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 30, meaning the home costs 30 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Devanahalli?

About ₹55,541 a month on a ₹64 L loan (20% down on a ₹80 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹36,874 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Devanahalli?

About 10.3% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
