The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About New Town AA-Block

Action Area I is the most developed part of New Town (Rajarhat), Kolkata's planned smart city east of Salt Lake. It has wide roads, planned housing blocks, Eco Park nearby and quick access to Sector V's IT offices and the airport. Many large apartment complexes are fully occupied.

  • Planned smart city
  • Close to Sector V IT hub
  • Eco Park
  • Wide roads
  • Near airport

Major employers nearby: Salt Lake Sector V IT offices, DLF IT Park & offices in New Town, TCS, Wipro & Cognizant campuses.

Getting around

  • Metro: Orange Line (New Garia–Airport) through New Town under construction; Green Line: Sector V ~4 km
  • Highway: Major Arterial Road; Biswa Bangla Sarani; VIP Road to the airport
  • Peak traffic: Major Arterial Road and Biswa Bangla Sarani are generally free-flowing, slower at peaks
  • Congestion: Low

Schools

  • Delhi Public School, New Town (CBSE) — Large CBSE school
  • Schools in New Town & Salt Lake (Various) — Many CBSE and ICSE options

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Tata Medical Center, Hospitals in New Town & Salt Lake
  • Malls: Axis Mall, City Centre 2
  • Parks: Eco Park, Mother's Wax Museum area

Investment view

Action Area I is the most settled part of New Town, with steady demand from IT staff. Price growth has been better than older Kolkata. 2BHK rents run about ₹18,000–22,000/mo. Good for buyers working in Sector V or New Town.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in New Town AA-Block?

Even if prices keep rising at New Town AA-Block’s 10-year trend of 7.3% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.28 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.4% a year. New Town AA-Block’s 10-year trend is 2.1 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹70 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in New Town AA-Block?

About 3.4% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹20,000 a month on a home worth about ₹70 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 29, meaning the home costs 29 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in New Town AA-Block?

About ₹48,598 a month on a ₹56 L loan (20% down on a ₹70 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹31,515 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in New Town AA-Block?

About 7.3% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.1% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
