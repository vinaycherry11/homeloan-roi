The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Salt Lake Sec I–IV

Salt Lake (Bidhannagar) Sectors I to III are the original planned residential blocks of Salt Lake, with independent houses, cooperative housing and quiet tree-lined lanes. The Green Line metro now links Salt Lake to Sealdah and Howrah, and Sector V's IT offices are close by.

  • Close to Sector V
  • Quiet lanes
  • Established neighbourhood

Major employers nearby: Salt Lake Sector V IT offices, State government offices, Bidhannagar businesses.

Investment view

Salt Lake is a stable, sought-after address, but price growth has been slow because most homes are resale and supply is limited. 2BHK rents run about ₹20,000–25,000/mo. Best for families who want a calm, central-east location.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Salt Lake Sec I–IV?

Even if prices keep rising at Salt Lake Sec I–IV’s 10-year trend of 4.5% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹2.78 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.7% a year. Salt Lake Sec I–IV’s 10-year trend is 5.2 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹75 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Salt Lake Sec I–IV?

About 3.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹22,000 a month on a home worth about ₹75 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 28, meaning the home costs 28 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Salt Lake Sec I–IV?

About ₹52,069 a month on a ₹60 L loan (20% down on a ₹75 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹33,194 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Salt Lake Sec I–IV?

About 4.5% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 3.4% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
