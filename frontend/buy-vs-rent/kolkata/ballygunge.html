The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Ballygunge

Ballygunge is Kolkata's most prestigious old-money residential address — a leafy, elite neighbourhood south of Park Street known for large heritage houses, exclusive clubs, premier educational institutions, and a refined Bengali cultural milieu. Property prices at ₹15,000–18,000/sqft rival South Mumbai or South Delhi values. New supply is near-zero and the area has maintained its prestige for over a century.

  • Kolkata's most prestigious address
  • Heritage houses & elite clubs
  • Top school belt (La Martinière, South Point)
  • Very limited new supply
  • Cultural & diplomatic community hub

Major employers nearby: Park Street–Camac Street commercial offices (2 km), Government of West Bengal Secretariat (3 km), Corporate offices in nearby Alipore, Medical institutions (SSKM Hospital nearby).

Getting around

  • Metro: Rabindra Sarobar Station (Blue Line) — 1 km; Jatin Das Park Station — 1.5 km; Blue Line direct to Esplanade and Park Street
  • Highway: EM Bypass — 4 km; Alipore Road — 2 km; Hazra Road — 0.5 km
  • Peak traffic: Moderate; Ballygunge Circular Road and Gariahat crossing can slow during market hours; generally less chaotic than north Kolkata
  • Congestion: Moderate

Schools

  • South Point High School (CBSE) — One of Kolkata's most renowned schools; established 1970; very high demand
  • La Martinière for Boys (ISC/ICSE) — Historic 1836-era elite school; one of India's most prestigious institutions
  • Calcutta International School (IB/Cambridge) — Top IB school in Kolkata; adheres to international standards

Nearby

  • Hospitals: SSKM Hospital (2 km), Woodlands Medical Centre (2 km), Peerless Hospital (3 km)
  • Malls: Gariahat Market (1 km), Acropolis Mall (4 km), Forum Mall Elgin Road (3 km)
  • Parks: Rabindra Sarobar Lake & Park (1 km), Victoria Memorial Gardens (3 km), Minto Park (2 km)

Investment view

Ballygunge is Kolkata's premium capital preservation play — 7–9% appreciation with structural scarcity. Prices at ₹15,000–18,000/sqft are Kolkata's highest. Rental yields at 3–4% are modest but tenant quality is exceptional. Near-zero new supply means prices here rarely decline. Best for HNI long-term holders and NRIs seeking a prestigious Kolkata address.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Ballygunge?

Even if prices keep rising at Ballygunge’s 10-year trend of 6.4% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹4.22 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.5% a year. Ballygunge’s 10-year trend is 3.1 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.65 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Ballygunge?

About 3.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹48,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.65 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 29, meaning the home costs 29 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Ballygunge?

About ₹1,14,553 a month on a ₹1.32 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.65 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹73,428 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Ballygunge?

About 6.4% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.3% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
