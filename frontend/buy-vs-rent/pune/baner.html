The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Baner

Baner is Pune's most upscale mid-city residential address, popular with senior IT professionals and startup founders who want proximity to both Hinjewadi and the old city. It has a vibrant restaurant and café strip, premium apartment stock, and relatively low density compared to Koregaon Park. Baner-Balewadi-Aundh is a contiguous premium belt with seamless connectivity to the Hinjewadi IT corridor.

  • Premium Hinjewadi fringe address
  • Vibrant F&B & café culture
  • Senior IT & startup professional belt
  • Low density premium apartments
  • Metro Line 3 corridor (planned)

Major employers nearby: Hinjewadi Phase 1 IT park (5 km), Westfield IT park Baner (local), Infosys BPO Baner, L&T Technology Services, Persistent Systems (Senapati Bapat Road).

Getting around

  • Metro: Baner and Baner Gaon stations on Metro Line 3 (Pink Line) under construction; expected 2026
  • Highway: Mumbai–Pune Expressway via Wakad — 6 km; NH-48 — 5 km
  • Peak traffic: Heavy at Baner Road junction and Pashan–Sus Road crossing 8–10 am and 6–9 pm
  • Congestion: High

Nearby

  • Malls: Westend Mall Aundh (3 km), Xion Mall Wakad (5 km), Balewadi High Street (2 km)

Investment view

Baner delivers 10–13% appreciation as a premium Hinjewadi corridor address. Rental yields at 4–4.8% are solid with 2BHK rents of ₹28,000–50,000/mo. Metro Line 3 (Hinjewadi to Civil Court via Baner) is the biggest near-term catalyst. One of Pune's top balanced investment localities.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Baner?

Even if prices keep rising at Baner’s 10-year trend of 7.8% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹41.2 L richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.3% a year. Baner’s 10-year trend is 0.5 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.00 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Baner?

About 4.4% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹37,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.00 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 23, meaning the home costs 23 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Baner?

About ₹69,426 a month on a ₹80 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.00 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹36,593 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Baner?

About 7.8% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.0% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
