The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Kalyani Nagar

Kalyani Nagar is a premium east Pune neighbourhood next to Koregaon Park, with wide roads, cafés, restaurants and a mix of older societies and new luxury towers. It is close to the airport, the Nagar Road IT parks and Kharadi, and now has its own metro station.

  • Premium address
  • Restaurants & nightlife
  • Close to Pune airport
  • Metro access
  • Near Koregaon Park

Major employers nearby: EON IT Park & World Trade Center, Kharadi, Offices on Nagar Road, Koregaon Park businesses.

Getting around

  • Metro: Aqua Line: Kalyani Nagar station on Nagar Road
  • Highway: Nagar Road (SH-27) ~1 km; Pune airport ~5 km
  • Peak traffic: Nagar Road and the Kalyani Nagar–Koregaon Park bridge are congested in the 9–11 am and 6–9 pm peaks
  • Congestion: Moderate

Schools

  • Bishop's Co-Ed School, Kalyani Nagar (ICSE) — Well-known school in the area
  • Symbiosis International School, Viman Nagar (IB/IGCSE) — International school nearby

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Columbia Asia (Manipal) Kharadi (~5 km), Sahyadri Hospital, Nagar Road
  • Malls: Phoenix Marketcity, Viman Nagar (~2 km), Koregaon Park restaurants (~3 km)
  • Parks: Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary area, Koregaon Park lanes

Investment view

Kalyani Nagar holds value well thanks to its address, airport proximity and metro. Rental demand from senior IT staff and expatriates is steady. 2BHK rents run about ₹32,000–38,000/mo. Best for buyers who want an established premium east Pune address.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Kalyani Nagar?

Even if prices keep rising at Kalyani Nagar’s 10-year trend of 7.8% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.94 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.5% a year. Kalyani Nagar’s 10-year trend is 1.7 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.25 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Kalyani Nagar?

About 3.4% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹35,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.25 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 30, meaning the home costs 30 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Kalyani Nagar?

About ₹86,782 a month on a ₹1.00 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.25 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹56,991 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Kalyani Nagar?

About 7.8% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.0% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
