The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Koregaon Park

Koregaon Park is Pune's most prestigious and cosmopolitan residential address, known for its shaded avenues, colonial bungalows, luxury apartments, top restaurants, and international culture. It is home to the Osho International Meditation Resort, a major international draw. Housing supply is extremely limited and prices are among Pune's highest. Primarily an HNI, expat, and luxury lifestyle market.

  • Pune's most prestigious address
  • Osho International Resort landmark
  • Luxury bungalow & apartment zone
  • Expat & HNI community
  • Iconic dining & nightlife belt

Major employers nearby: Pune CBD offices (Senapati Bapat Rd — 6 km), BPO cluster (Bund Garden Road), Pune Cantonment establishments, NGOs & international organisations (Osho ecosystem).

Investment view

Koregaon Park is Pune's premier capital preservation play at 7–8% appreciation with very limited new supply. Rental yields are modest at 3.5–4% but tenant quality is exceptional (expats, HNIs, senior executives). Best for long-term luxury holding rather than yield-driven investment.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Koregaon Park?

Even if prices keep rising at Koregaon Park’s 10-year trend of 7.8% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.69 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.1% a year. Koregaon Park’s 10-year trend is 1.3 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.50 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Koregaon Park?

About 3.8% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹47,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.50 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 27, meaning the home costs 27 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Koregaon Park?

About ₹1,04,139 a month on a ₹1.20 Cr loan (20% down on a ₹1.50 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹63,389 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Koregaon Park?

About 7.8% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.0% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
