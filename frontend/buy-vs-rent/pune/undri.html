The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Undri

Undri is a south-east Pune suburb past Kondhwa and NIBM Road. It has many newer mid-segment projects, international schools and a quieter setting than NIBM, at lower prices. Roads and public transport are still improving.

  • Mid-segment projects
  • Good schools
  • Near NIBM Road
  • Quieter setting
  • Value pricing

Major employers nearby: Magarpatta City & SP Infocity (~8 km), Offices in Camp & Hadapsar, Kondhwa businesses.

Getting around

  • Metro: No metro nearby; the Swargate–Katraj extension is ~6–7 km away
  • Highway: Pune–Saswad road ~3 km; Katraj–Kondhwa Road ~3 km
  • Peak traffic: The Undri–NIBM road and Kondhwa junctions slow down in peaks; narrow stretches remain
  • Congestion: Moderate

Schools

  • Bishop's School, Undri (ICSE) — Branch of the well-known Pune school
  • Delhi Public School, Pune (NIBM area) (CBSE) — Popular CBSE school nearby

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Inamdar Multispeciality Hospital (~4 km), Hospitals in Wanowrie & Camp (~8 km)
  • Malls: Dorabjee's Mall area (~4 km), Amanora Mall, Hadapsar (~10 km)
  • Parks: Hills around Undri, NIBM area greenery

Investment view

Undri gives school-going families newer homes at lower prices than NIBM, but the area is not on a major job corridor. 2BHK rents run about ₹16,000–19,000/mo. A sensible self-use buy for south-east Pune families.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Undri?

Even if prices keep rising at Undri’s 10-year trend of 6.4% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.50 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.5% a year. Undri’s 10-year trend is 3.1 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹60 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Undri?

About 3.6% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹18,000 a month on a home worth about ₹60 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 28, meaning the home costs 28 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Undri?

About ₹41,656 a month on a ₹48 L loan (20% down on a ₹60 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹26,156 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Undri?

About 6.4% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.1% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
