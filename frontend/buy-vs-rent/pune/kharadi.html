The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Kharadi

Kharadi is Pune's fastest-growing eastern IT corridor, anchored by EON Free Zone IT Park, World Trade Centre Pune, and the emerging Weikfield IT Park. It has attracted large GCCs and product companies and offers newer residential stock than older Pune suburbs. Kharadi sits between Viman Nagar (airport) and Hadapsar, giving it excellent connectivity in all directions. Property prices have risen ~12% in 2023–24.

  • EON Free Zone & WTC IT hub
  • Fastest-growing Pune IT corridor
  • Airport proximity
  • New residential stock
  • GCC and product company belt

Major employers nearby: Zensar Technologies (HQ), Cybage Software, KPIT Technologies, Barclays Technology Centre, Credit Suisse (now UBS) GCC.

Investment view

Kharadi has delivered ~12% appreciation in 2023–24 backed by strong GCC demand. Average prices at ~₹9,174/sqft with 4.5–5% rental yields. Phase 2 metro extension to Kharadi, if confirmed, will be a large price catalyst. Strong fundamentals make it one of Pune's best mid-term investment bets.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Kharadi?

If prices keep rising at Kharadi’s 10-year trend of 9.0% a year, buying leaves you about ₹84.6 L richer after 20 years than renting and investing the difference. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 7.9% a year. Kharadi’s 10-year trend is 1.1 points above that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹90 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Kharadi?

About 4.4% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹33,000 a month on a home worth about ₹90 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 23, meaning the home costs 23 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Kharadi?

About ₹62,483 a month on a ₹72 L loan (20% down on a ₹90 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹33,233 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Kharadi?

About 9.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 6.0% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
