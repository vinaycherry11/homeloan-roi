The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Wagholi

Wagholi is on the Pune–Ahmednagar highway, just beyond Kharadi on Pune's east side. Lower prices than Kharadi and Viman Nagar have made it one of the city's busiest affordable housing markets, with many large projects. The main highway is often congested, and local infrastructure is still catching up.

  • Affordable housing
  • Large township supply
  • Close to Kharadi IT
  • Pune–Ahmednagar highway
  • Educational institutes

Major employers nearby: EON IT Park & WTC, Kharadi, Offices on Nagar Road, Ranjangaon MIDC (further out).

Getting around

  • Metro: Aqua Line ends at Ramwadi (~8–10 km); an extension towards Wagholi has been proposed
  • Highway: Pune–Ahmednagar Highway (SH-27/NH-753F) runs through; Ring Road planned
  • Peak traffic: The Pune–Ahmednagar highway through Wagholi is badly congested in morning and evening peaks
  • Congestion: High

Schools

  • Lexicon International School, Wagholi (CBSE) — Large local school
  • Other CBSE schools along the highway (CBSE) — Several options in Wagholi and Kharadi

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Hospitals in Kharadi (~6 km), Local multispecialty hospitals
  • Malls: Phoenix Marketcity, Viman Nagar (~10 km), Local retail along the highway
  • Parks: Wagholi lake area

Investment view

Wagholi is a value market: homes cost far less than Kharadi, but heavy supply and traffic have kept growth moderate. 2BHK rents run about ₹14,000–17,000/mo. Fits first-time buyers working in east Pune who are comfortable with a highway commute.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Wagholi?

Even if prices keep rising at Wagholi’s 10-year trend of 6.7% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.28 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.5% a year. Wagholi’s 10-year trend is 2.8 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹55 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Wagholi?

About 3.5% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹16,000 a month on a home worth about ₹55 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 29, meaning the home costs 29 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Wagholi?

About ₹38,184 a month on a ₹44 L loan (20% down on a ₹55 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹24,476 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Wagholi?

About 6.7% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.5% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
