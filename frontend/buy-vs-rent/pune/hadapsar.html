The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Hadapsar

Hadapsar is Pune's largest eastern suburb and a major IT and industrial hub, anchored by Magarpatta City — a self-contained integrated township with IT parks, residential towers, schools, and hospitals. It connects to the Pune–Solapur Road and has good access to the airport. The area offers a wide range of housing from affordable to premium within the Magarpatta ecosystem.

  • Magarpatta City integrated township
  • Pune–Solapur Road corridor
  • Large IT and BPO presence
  • Self-contained township infrastructure
  • Mix of affordable and premium housing

Major employers nearby: Magarpatta Cybercity IT Park (Wipro, Cognizant, HSBC GCC), Amanora Business District, Finolex Industries, Mercedes-Benz India (Chakan nearby).

Investment view

Hadapsar has seen 8–10% appreciation annually driven by Magarpatta Cybercity demand. Rental yields at 3.5–4.5% are steady. Magarpatta City township commands a premium for its self-contained infrastructure. Good for investors seeking township-model stability with moderate entry prices.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Hadapsar?

Even if prices keep rising at Hadapsar’s 10-year trend of 7.3% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹95.7 L richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.9% a year. Hadapsar’s 10-year trend is 1.6 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹70 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Hadapsar?

About 3.9% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹23,000 a month on a home worth about ₹70 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 25, meaning the home costs 25 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Hadapsar?

About ₹48,598 a month on a ₹56 L loan (20% down on a ₹70 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹28,515 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Hadapsar?

About 7.3% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.8% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
