The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Kondhwa

Kondhwa is a large, established south Pune area along the Kondhwa–NIBM corridor, close to Camp and Wanowrie. It mixes older housing with newer societies and has busy markets, schools and hospitals. Prices are moderate for an area this close to the city.

  • Established neighbourhood
  • NIBM Road corridor
  • Close to Camp
  • Busy local markets
  • Mid-segment housing

Major employers nearby: Offices in Camp & Hadapsar, Magarpatta City (~8 km), Local businesses & trade.

Getting around

  • Metro: No direct metro; nearest is the Swargate area or the Katraj extension (under construction), ~6 km
  • Highway: Katraj–Kondhwa Road; Pune–Satara road via Katraj
  • Peak traffic: Kondhwa Road and the Khadi Machine Chowk stretch are congested at peak hours
  • Congestion: High

Schools

  • Delhi Public School, Pune (NIBM area) (CBSE) — Popular CBSE school
  • Vibgyor High, NIBM (CBSE/ICSE) — K-12 school nearby

Nearby

  • Hospitals: Inamdar Multispeciality Hospital (local), Ruby Hall Clinic, Wanowrie (~5 km)
  • Malls: Dorabjee's Mall, NIBM (~3 km), Camp shopping (~6 km)
  • Parks: NIBM hill areas

Investment view

Kondhwa is a well-served but crowded neighbourhood; price growth has been steady rather than fast. 2BHK rents run about ₹16,000–19,000/mo. Works for buyers who want city access and amenities at moderate prices.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Kondhwa?

Even if prices keep rising at Kondhwa’s 10-year trend of 6.0% a year, renting and investing the money you would have spent on the home leaves you about ₹1.64 Cr richer after 20 years. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 9.5% a year. Kondhwa’s 10-year trend is 3.5 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹60 L, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Kondhwa?

About 3.6% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹18,000 a month on a home worth about ₹60 L. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 28, meaning the home costs 28 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Kondhwa?

About ₹41,656 a month on a ₹48 L loan (20% down on a ₹60 L home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹26,156 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Kondhwa?

About 6.0% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 4.1% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
