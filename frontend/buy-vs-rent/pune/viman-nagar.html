The 20-year comparison

The buyer pays the down payment and stamp duty on day 1, then the EMI and maintenance each month. The renter puts the same day-1 money into a Nifty 50 index fund, pays rent, and each month invests whatever the buyer spends over rent. Whoever spends less in a month invests the difference, so both start and continue with the same money.

About Viman Nagar

Viman Nagar is a premium residential and commercial suburb adjacent to Pune International Airport, attracting frequent flyers, senior executives, and expats. It sits between Koregaon Park (lifestyle) and Kharadi (IT hub), giving residents the best of both worlds. Giga Space IT Park and proximity to Kharadi's EON Zone drive IT worker demand. Property stock is a mix of premium apartments and commercial towers.

  • Airport-adjacent premium address
  • Koregaon Park & Kharadi proximity
  • Expat & senior executive zone
  • Giga Space IT Park
  • Premium lifestyle infrastructure

Major employers nearby: Giga Space IT Park (local), Tech Mahindra (Viman Nagar), Global Step, Kharadi IT offices (4 km), Pune Airport aerotropolis tenants.

Investment view

Viman Nagar delivers 8–10% appreciation at premium price points backed by airport adjacency and dual employment catchment (Giga Space + Kharadi). Rental yields at 4–4.5% with high tenant quality. Best for investors targeting expats and senior executives who value airport proximity.

Questions people ask

Is it better to buy or rent in Viman Nagar?

At Viman Nagar’s 10-year price trend of 8.1% a year, the two choices end up within ₹27.7 L of each other after 20 years (renting slightly ahead). Small changes in price growth, rent or your loan rate decide it. Buying wins only if prices grow faster than 8.4% a year. Viman Nagar’s 10-year trend is 0.3 points below that. This assumes a typical 2BHK at about ₹1.05 Cr, 8.5% home loan for 20 years and a Nifty 50 index fund at 12% a year for the renter.

What is the rental yield in Viman Nagar?

About 4.2% gross for a typical 2BHK: roughly ₹37,000 a month on a home worth about ₹1.05 Cr. That is a price-to-rent ratio of 24, meaning the home costs 24 years of rent.

What is the EMI on a 2BHK in Viman Nagar?

About ₹72,897 a month on a ₹84 L loan (20% down on a ₹1.05 Cr home) at 8.5% for 20 years. In year 1, owning costs about ₹40,272 a month more than renting the same flat, after maintenance.

How fast have property prices grown in Viman Nagar?

About 8.1% a year on average over 2014–2024, with rents growing about 5.4% a year (Blended 10-yr trend (2014–2024) · RBI House Price Index, Anarock Research, 99acres, Knight Frank). Past growth is not a promise of future growth.
