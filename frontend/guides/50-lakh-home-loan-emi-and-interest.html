₹50 lakh home loan: EMI, total interest and what you really pay

By Sri Naga Vinay Mutyala · Updated November 2026

A ₹50 lakh home loan at 8.5% for 20 years has an EMI of ₹43,391. Over the full term you pay about ₹54.1 L in interest, so the loan costs ₹1.04 Cr in total.

In the first year about 81% of every EMI is interest. That is why prepaying early saves the most, and why the loan barely shrinks in the first few years.

Each 0.5% on the rate changes the EMI by roughly ₹1,500–1,600 a month and total interest by about ₹4 lakh. It is worth negotiating, and switching lenders if your rate is well above new-borrower rates.

Costs on top of the loan

  • Down payment: banks lend up to 75–90% of the property value, so a ₹50 lakh loan usually goes with a ₹60–65 lakh home and a ₹10–15 lakh down payment.
  • Stamp duty and registration: roughly 5–8% of the property value depending on the state, paid upfront and not covered by the loan.
  • Processing fee, insurance and maintenance add more.

Questions people ask

What is the EMI for a ₹50 lakh home loan?

At 8.5% for 20 years, about ₹43,391 a month. At 8% it is ₹41,822; at 9% it is ₹44,986.

How much interest will I pay on a ₹50 lakh home loan?

About ₹54.1 L over 20 years at 8.5%, so you repay ₹1.04 Cr in total, 2.1 times what you borrowed.

What salary do I need for a ₹50 lakh home loan?

Banks usually keep all EMIs under about 40–50% of take-home pay. An EMI of ₹43,391 suggests a take-home income of roughly ₹96,425 a month or more, before other loans.
